Text · opsz 18

Sun Serif has two optical sizes. The text size has sturdy stems, open counters and an even colour, so that a paragraph stays calm at 18 px. The display size takes over from 21 px. A true italic follows the roman in x-height and width, and the weights run from Regular to Bold on one axis.
